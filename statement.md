Problem Statement:

​In urgent or emergency situations, individuals often lack the time or capacity to manually contact multiple family members or guardians with critical location and status updates.
The existing reliance on slow, one-by-one communication can lead to significant delays in receiving help and cause unnecessary anxiety among contacts. 
A solution is needed for rapid, simultaneous dissemination of emergency information.

Scope of the Project:

​The Emergency Alert System (EAS) v1.0 addresses this problem by providing a simple, localized CLI tool.
​Included: Basic contact management (store name/number), pre-set and custom message drafting, and activity logging.
​Excluded (Future Scope): Actual SMS/API integration (sending real messages), graphical user interface (GUI), persistent database storage (currently contacts are lost on program exit), and location tracking.
 
Target Users:

​The system is primarily designed for:
​Students/Individuals who need a quick, simple way to notify a small circle of trusted contacts (family, roommates, close friends) during an emergency.
​Users who are developing or learning command-line applications and need a basic, functional prototype for notification services.

High-Level Features:

​Instant Mass Notification: The ability to send a pre-defined alert to all contacts simultaneously (simulated).
​User-Friendly Menu Interface: A clear, numbered menu for quick navigation and command execution.
​Audit Trail: Automated logging of the sender and message content to provide a record of all triggered alerts.
